## Overview

Lumin brings architectural aluminium into spaces shaped by daylight, proportion and a connection to the outdoors. Considered framing helps give a view its place in a room while complementing the lines of the building.

For homes and architectural projects, discuss the intended opening sizes, appearance and glazing requirements with Alumex. Profile selection, finishes and installation details should be reviewed together to achieve a coherent result suited to the project.

## Specifications

### Architectural Integration

- Review the framing arrangement alongside the building’s elevations and interior layout.
- Coordinate sightlines, junctions and visible frame proportions.
- Discuss the intended balance between fixed glazing and operable openings.

### Materials & Appearance

- Aluminium profiles and surface finishes selected around the project’s design direction.
- Review available colour and finish options with the Alumex team.
- Coordinate hardware, glazing and surrounding materials for a consistent appearance.

### Project Performance

- Specify daylight, comfort, security and weather requirements before selecting a configuration.
- Review glazing, fixing and drainage details as part of the complete system.
- Confirm tested performance, dimensions and applicable documentation for the chosen product.

## Benefits

- Frame views and bring daylight into architectural spaces.
- Coordinate aluminium detailing with a project’s wider material palette.
- Support design discussions for homes, hospitality spaces and commercial interiors.
- Review the relationship between framing, glazing and usable space.
- Choose finishes that complement the building’s character.
- Plan installation junctions early in the design process.
- Develop the final specification with product-specific technical guidance.
