## Overview

Industrial aluminium solutions start with the relationship between a profile and the job it needs to do. From support frameworks and protective enclosures to fabricated assemblies, the right extrusion brings structure, consistency and practical integration to a project.

Alumex works with project teams to discuss profile geometry, surface treatment and fabrication requirements. Share your drawings, intended operating conditions and assembly details so the appropriate profile and finishing approach can be reviewed before production.

## Specifications

### Profiles & Integration

- Extruded aluminium sections for industrial frameworks, enclosures and fabricated components.
- Profile geometry selected around fixing points, interfaces and the intended assembly.
- Drawing-led discussions for projects requiring a particular cross-section.

### Finishing & Fabrication

- Discuss mill-finish, anodized or powder-coated surfaces according to the intended application.
- Identify cutting, drilling and other value-addition requirements during project review.
- Confirm dimensional tolerances, material requirements and inspection criteria before ordering.

### Project Requirements

- Provide operating environment, loading requirements and relevant drawings for technical review.
- Agree profile dimensions, finish, quantities and delivery requirements with the team.
- Application-specific performance and material documentation should be confirmed for the selected profile.

## Benefits

- Coordinate profile and assembly design from the start of the project.
- Specify fixing and connection details that suit the installation.
- Choose surface treatments around appearance and the operating environment.
- Support repeatable fabrication through agreed drawings and requirements.
- Use in equipment frameworks, protective enclosures, workstations and other engineered assemblies, subject to technical review.
- Discuss repeat-order requirements and project scheduling with the Alumex team.
