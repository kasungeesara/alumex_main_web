## Overview

A home needs aluminium systems that work with the way people live. Household solutions bring together windows, doors, screens and outdoor features, helping connect rooms with natural light, fresh air and the spaces beyond.

Discuss your opening sizes, room layouts and preferred finishes with Alumex. Profile, glazing and hardware choices can then be coordinated around the architecture and practical needs of your home.

## Specifications

### Designed for Home

- Discuss windows, doors and outdoor aluminium features for your space.
- Review access, ventilation and opening configurations room by room.
- Coordinate framing with existing walls and surrounding finishes.

### Finishes & Details

- Explore available anodized and powder-coated finishes.
- Match hardware and glazing to the selected framing system.
- Confirm colours, dimensions and component availability before ordering.

### Planning & Installation

- Share plans or measurements for project-specific guidance.
- Review drainage, fixing and threshold details with the installation team.
- Confirm applicable performance documentation for the selected system.

## Benefits

- Bring natural light into everyday living spaces.
- Connect interiors with gardens, balconies and courtyards.
- Coordinate aluminium finishes across a new home or renovation.
- Select opening arrangements around furniture and circulation.
- Discuss practical maintenance and installation requirements early.
