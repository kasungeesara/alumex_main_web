## Overview

Alumex architectural aluminium doors bring together visual clarity and practical performance for homes, commercial developments and public-facing projects. The range can support sleek contemporary entrances, restrained glazed openings and more classic architectural compositions, allowing the door system to complement the wider design language of the building.

Door dimensions, frame profiles, glazing, finishes and hardware can be coordinated around the project rather than treated as isolated components. Explore completed work in the [project portfolio](/projects), then discuss the drawings, opening sizes, site exposure and performance requirements with the Alumex team before final selection.

## Specifications

### Door Designs & Configurations

- Aluminium door designs for residential, commercial and architectural applications.
- Configurations can be developed around the opening size, circulation needs and desired visual character.
- Coordinate doors with adjacent windows, fixed glazing and framing for a consistent elevation.

### Profiles, Finishes & Hardware

- Select profiles and finishes to suit the project geometry and architectural palette.
- Review handles, locks, seals, rollers and other hardware as part of the complete door system.
- Aluminium construction offers dimensional stability and resistance to warping, cracking and fading under normal use.

### Durability & Comfort

- Corrosion-resistant aluminium supports long service life with comparatively low maintenance.
- Suitable glazing, seals and frame configurations can improve thermal performance and indoor comfort.
- Final energy, weather and security performance depends on the complete specified and installed assembly.

### Technical Review

- Provide drawings, opening dimensions, intended use and environmental conditions.
- Confirm structural, weather, security and accessibility requirements for each door application.
- Agree fabrication, testing, documentation and installation details before final specification.

## Benefits

- Adapt door dimensions, configurations, glazing and finishes to the project.
- Create a cohesive transition between interior and exterior spaces.
- Support contemporary or classic architectural styles with a refined aluminium finish.
- Benefit from corrosion resistance, dimensional stability and low maintenance.
- Improve comfort and potential energy performance with an appropriately specified frame, glazing and sealing system.
- Use coordinated aluminium doors across residential, commercial and public-facing applications.
