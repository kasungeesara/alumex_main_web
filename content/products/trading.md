## Overview

Reliable project supply begins with a clear specification. The Trading offering supports discussions around aluminium materials and systems for contractors, fabricators and businesses, connecting the requirements of a project with a suitable supply enquiry.

Share the product description, dimensions, finish, quantities and delivery location with Alumex. The team can review available options, identify the information needed for a quotation and help coordinate the next steps for procurement.

## Specifications

### Product Selection

- Discuss the aluminium products and systems required for your business or project.
- Provide drawings, dimensions, finish references or sample information where available.
- Confirm the exact product specification and current availability with the team.

### Order Coordination

- Request a quotation based on quantities and clearly defined requirements.
- Confirm packaging, delivery location and any handling considerations.
- Agree lead times and order conditions before making procurement commitments.

### Project Support

- Coordinate material requirements with your fabrication or installation programme.
- Discuss repeat requirements and phased project deliveries where needed.
- Request relevant product documentation and confirm suitability for the intended application.

## Benefits

- Create a clear brief for pricing and procurement.
- Compare supply options against the same product requirements.
- Coordinate finishes, quantities and project documentation.
- Plan material handling and delivery around site needs.
- Support purchasing enquiries for fabricators, contractors and commercial projects.
- Discuss repeat orders and project supply with one enquiry point.
