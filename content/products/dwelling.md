## Overview

Thoughtfully designed aluminium windows and doors shape the way a home feels every day. They frame the view, bring daylight into living spaces and help connect interiors with gardens, balconies and courtyards.

The Dwelling range brings the conversation back to your space: the size of an opening, how a room is used, the available clearance and the finish that complements the architecture. Discuss your plans with Alumex to identify a suitable framing and glazing arrangement.

## Specifications

### Openings & Layout

- Discuss window and door configurations around the layout of each room.
- Review opening direction, access, ventilation and available clearance.
- Coordinate frames with surrounding walls, thresholds and interior finishes.

### Frames & Finishes

- Aluminium framing selected to complement the architectural design.
- Discuss powder-coated or anodized finishes and available colours with the team.
- Coordinate glazing, seals and hardware with the selected system.

### Comfort & Installation

- Specify glazing according to the project’s daylight, privacy and comfort requirements.
- Confirm weather performance and security requirements for the selected configuration.
- Final opening sizes, drainage and fixing details should be checked during installation planning.

## Benefits

- Create visual connections between living spaces and the outdoors.
- Plan natural light and ventilation around the needs of each room.
- Coordinate window and door finishes across a home.
- Choose opening configurations that work with furniture and circulation.
- Suitable for discussions around new homes, renovations, apartments and residential developments.
- Review glazing and hardware choices as part of a complete system.
- Get project-specific guidance before finalizing dimensions and installation details.
